Technical Bottlenecks & Practical Solutions

  • Folder Virtualization vs Git Trees: Git tracks file contents rather than directory nodes. To establish the target folder architecture (img/) directly via remote management, empty directory preservation rules were adopted before staging high-resolution graphic deliverables.
  • Cross-Platform File System Case-Sensitivity: Windows operating systems exhibit case-insensitivity, whereas GitHub Linux runners enforce strict POSIX case-sensitivity. Assets labeled with capitalization mismatches failed during initial client requests. Standardizing strict lowercase extensions and exact string references permanently mitigated broken links.
  • Cache Invalidation & CDN Latency: Aggressive browser caching caused recently pushed commits to render previous CSS stylesheet definitions. Bypassing client caches via hard refreshes (Ctrl + F5) and validating the GitHub Actions execution log allowed us to verify correct asset delivery in real-time.

2. Project Presentation & AI-Assisted Concept Prototyping

Kurimanya Kids Vision: Closing the Pediatric Healthcare Gap

Congenital talipes equinovarus (clubfoot) is one of the most disabling musculoskeletal deformities when left untreated or poorly managed. Although the Ponseti method achieves initial clinical correction rates above 90%, the maintenance phase demands strict adherence to an abduction orthosis for up to 4 or 5 years. In Ecuador, treatment abandonment rates remain alarmingly high due to imported brace expenses ($250–$400 USD per set), rapid child growth cycles requiring full brace replacements every 6 months, and complete absence of quantitative compliance feedback for parents and physicians.

Kurimanya Kids addresses these three bottlenecks through an open-source, modular smart orthosis fabricated locally using FabLab Cuenca infrastructure (FDM additive manufacturing in technical PETG/TPU, laser-cut anatomical straps, and embedded telemetry microelectronics).

3. Feasibility Analysis & National Healthcare Data (INEC)

Public Health Reality: Morbidity & Physical Disability Landscape in Ecuador

Biomedical engineering projects must be firmly substantiated by epidemiological metrics. We extracted and examined the official National Hospital Discharges and Disability Registry (INEC Ecuador, 2024), specifically table Cuadro No. 3.1.18: Número de egresos por discapacidad al momento del ingreso del paciente, según provincia de residencia.

The dataset highlights critical geographic concentrations of motor/physical disabilities, justifying the establishment of regional digital fabrication hubs for assistive health devices:

Total Hospital Discharges 1,130,000+ Total inpatient events (2024)
Azuay (Local Ground) 793 Physical disability discharges
Pichincha (Sierra Hub) 720 Physical disability discharges
Guayas (Coastal Hub) 475 Physical disability discharges

When combined with the specific clubfoot registry (ICD-10 code Q66: 481 national cases), these records demonstrate that the southern Austro region (Azuay and surrounding provinces) accounts for a high proportion of physical motor conditions requiring specialized orthopedic orthoses. Developing open-source digital manufacturing templates at FabLab Cuenca offers a viable alternative to alleviate the logistical strain placed on tertiary healthcare centers.
