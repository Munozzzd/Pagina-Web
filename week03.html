Overhang & Bridging Test: 3D Snoopy Figure

To evaluate cooling efficiency, overhang stability, and support structure removal quality, we printed a detailed 3D Snoopy figure. The model features steep angle cantilevers (>45°) under the snout, ears, and hands.

  • Surface Quality: Smooth layer deposition across organic curved surfaces with minimal layer lines.
  • Dimensional Accuracy: High fidelity on feature intersections (nose, ears, feet) with tight tolerances.
  • Support Breakdown: Tree supports (organic supports) were configured at a 40° threshold, allowing clean detachment without scarring.

Predefined Calibration Test Benchmarks (Tol3D / Tolerance Matrix)

We performed calibration matrix prints across FabLab printers using PLA (0.4 mm nozzle) to evaluate dimensional tolerance gaps ranging from 0.1mm to 0.5mm clearance. The CoreXY Bambu Lab achieved clean pin clearance at 0.15mm, whereas the Cartesian Ender 3 required at least 0.30mm clearance to prevent fusion.

Step-by-Step FDM Printing Workflow

  1. 3D CAD Modeling & STL Export: Generate watertight solid geometry in Fusion 360 and export as high-resolution STL/3MF.
  2. Slicer Configuration: Import into OrcaSlicer/PrusaSlicer, set 0.2mm layer height, 0.4mm nozzle diameter, Gyroid infill, and Tree supports.
  3. Bed Preparation & Calibration: Clean PEI spring steel plate with Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA 99%) and run automatic bed-leveling.
  4. Extrusion & Monitoring: Load 1.75mm PLA filament, verify first-layer squish, and monitor thermal stability during print execution.
  5. Post-Processing: Allow print bed to cool to room temperature, flex PEI plate to detach print, and remove support interfaces.

d & e. Point Cloud Processing to Watertight Solid Mesh

Raw optical 3D scans contain non-manifold holes, noise, and floating geometry. To convert the point cloud into a printable solid model, we utilized CR Studio combined with MeshLab / Fusion 360 Mesh Workbench following these steps:

  1. Point Cloud Registration & Align: Fuse multiple spatial scan frames into a unified coordinate system.
  2. Noise Filtering & Outlier Removal: Delete floating extraneous polygons around hair and background.
  3. Watertight Mesh Reconstruction (Hole Filling): Apply Poisson surface reconstruction to close open neck and head boundaries.
  4. Decimation & Solidification: Reduce polygon count while preserving facial geometry features, exporting a clean STL solid.

f, g & h. MSLA Resin Printing, Safety Sheet & Curing Process

The solid facial model of Samantha Vásquez was manufactured using a UV LCD / MSLA Resin Printer (Elegoo Mars / Anycubic Photon Series) with photopolymer resin for ultra-high detail.

g. Liquid Resin Chemical Handling & Safety Regulations (MSDS)

Uncured photopolymer resins contain irritants and toxic acrylic monomers. Strict FabLab safety compliance was enforced during the resin printing of Samantha's model:

  • Nitrile Gloves: Mandatory chemical-resistant gloves (Latex is porous to photopolymers).
  • Respirator Mask: Organic Vapor VOC filter mask worn while handling open resin vats.
  • UV Eye Protection: Safety goggles to prevent chemical splashes and shielding from 405nm UV light exposure.
  • Fume Extraction: Operation inside active ventilation hoods to vent Volatile Organic Compounds.

h. Post-Processing & UV Curing Workflow

  1. Part Detachment: Carefully remove the printed bust from the build plate using a plastic scraper.
  2. IPA Wash Station: Submerge part in 99% Isopropyl Alcohol (Wash & Cure Station) for 6 minutes to dissolve uncured liquid resin layer.
  3. Support Removal: Snip thin resin support trees while material remains slightly pliable.
  4. UV Post-Curing: Place model inside 405nm UV Curing Chamber for 5 minutes to complete cross-polymerization and reach full structural hardness.

Comparative Analysis: Resin (SLA/MSLA) vs. Filament (PLA FDM)

Technology Key Advantages Key Disadvantages Project Application (Kurimanya Kids)
PLA Filament (FDM)
  • Low material cost & zero chemical handling risk.
  • Large build volume & high structural impact resistance.
  • Supports functional technical polymers (PETG, TPU).
  • Visible layer lines (anisotropy along Z-axis).
  • Lower feature resolution for fine sensor enclosures.
 Ideal for orthotic foot shells, structural hinges, and dynamic sizing brackets.
Photopolymer Resin (SLA/MSLA)
  • Ultra-high isotropic surface resolution (0.025 mm).
  • Isotropic material properties and smooth finish.
  • Perfect representation of organic anatomical scans.
  • Requires strict PPE, IPA washing & UV post-curing.
  • Brittle material unless using expensive tough resins.
 Ideal for custom anatomical molds, miniature medical prototypes, and master casts.

Weekly Retrospective

¿Qué aprendí esta semana?

Aprendí a calibrar parámetros de laminado FDM (soportes en árbol, densidad de relleno gyroid), operar el escáner óptico Creality CR-SCAN 01 para digitalizar anatomía humana y ejecutar el proceso completo de lavado IPA y curado UV en resina fotopolímera.

¿En qué me equivoqué?

En la primera pasada del escáner facial realizamos movimientos demasiado rápidos que hicieron perder el rastreo (tracking loss) del CR-SCAN 01, y en la impresión de resina retiramos los soportes después del curado UV, lo que causó pequeñas marcas en la superficie.

¿Qué fue lo más difícil?

Procesar la nube de puntos del escaneo facial para cerrar huecos en zonas con cabello y convertirlos en un volumen sólido 100% estanco (watertight mesh) sin deformar los rasgos característicos del rostro de Samantha.

Conclusiones y Recomendaciones

Conclusiones

La combinación de escaneo 3D óptico e impresión aditiva (FDM y MSLA) valida la capacidad del equipo para personalizar la ortesis Kurimanya Kids según la anatomía individual del paciente. La impresora Bambu Lab CoreXY se consolida como el equipo FDM más eficiente para la fabricación de componentes mecánicos en PETG/TPU.

Recomendaciones

  • Remover siempre las estructuras de soporte en impresiones de resina antes de introducir la pieza a la cámara de curado UV.
  • Usar matizadores o spray antirreflejante al escanear zonas brillantes o cabello para evitar pérdidas de seguimiento en el Creality CR-SCAN 01.
  • Mantener ventilación activa y uso estricto de guantes de nitrilo durante todo el manejo de resina líquida y lavado con alcohol isopropílico.
